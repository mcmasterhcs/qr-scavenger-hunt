Rules & Troubleshooting

Move-In Welcome Zone

The Move-In Welcome Zone is located in the green space between Edwards Hall and Commons Building on Saturday, and in the Matthews-Moulton-Wallingford courtyard on Sunday. Join us for a raffle, photo booth and other activities to celebrate your arrival at McMaster!

QR Code Scavenger Hunt - Rules

  • There are 12 clues hidden around campus.
  • Each clue has a QR code that you must scan to complete the clue.
  • The QR code poster also has a text string that you can enter manually, if scanning doesn't work.
  • Complete all 12 clues and show your completed list to the raffle table for more ticket entries.
  • Your progress is saved automatically as you complete each clue.

Troubleshooting

If your camera won't open:

  • Close this web page and open your camera app.
  • Scan the QR code; it should show you a text string, or your phone should ask for you to search for a term. The poster should also say the term.
  • Manually add that term to the answer.
For the example below, "clue answer 1" would be the answer you'd put into the clue.
